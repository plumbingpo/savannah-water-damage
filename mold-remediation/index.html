Mold is more than an unsightly nuisance: it is a serious health hazard and a sign of underlying moisture problems that threaten the structure of your property. Children, older adults and anyone with a weakened immune system are hit hardest by exposure, which can bring headaches, allergic reactions, breathing trouble and worse. Savannah Restoration sends IICRC-certified technicians to homes and commercial buildings across Savannah, where they contain mold, remove it and keep it from returning under strict industry protocols.

Effective mold remediation goes far beyond scrubbing visible mold off surfaces. Done professionally, the work includes finding where the moisture comes from, containing the area so spores cannot cross-contaminate other rooms, taking out affected material under HEPA filtration, applying antimicrobials to what remains and correcting the moisture issue to prevent a return. Working this way removes the mold where it starts instead of covering it up temporarily.

First comes a full assessment of the property, frequently with air quality tests that identify the kinds of spores in the air and how concentrated they are. With that data we can build a remediation plan aimed at exactly what your property needs. Throughout the project we maintain negative air pressure containment, run HEPA air scrubbers and follow the IICRC S520 standard for mold remediation. A final verification step, which can include a clearance air test on request, shows that the space meets the standard for safe occupancy.

How to Prevent Mold Remediation Problems

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly mold issues down the road. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • Keep indoor humidity below 60% (ideally 30 to 50%) with dehumidifiers and proper ventilation
  • Clean and maintain your HVAC system and change filters regularly to prevent mold in ductwork
  • Address condensation on windows, pipes and cold surfaces promptly
  • Hang or launder wet fabrics promptly and wipe up spills as they happen
  • Mold-resistant paint and wallboard are the better choice wherever moisture is common, such as a basement or bath
  • Clear gutters and downspouts regularly, which keeps rain from soaking the foundation walls

Signs It Is Time for Mold Remediation

Catching these issues early helps you avoid costly problems and protect your property. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • Stains spreading on a ceiling or wall, or dark marks ringing the air vents
  • Discovering a plumbing or roof leak that has been dripping for a long time
  • Peeling wallpaper, bubbling paint or warped materials that point to hidden moisture
  • Recurring respiratory symptoms, allergies or headaches that improve when you are away from home
  • Closets, bathrooms or other damp, enclosed rooms that always smell musty
  • Black, green, white or other colored growth you can see on surfaces in the building
  • Water damage not long ago that was left to dry without professional equipment

What a Professional Brings to Mold Remediation

Mold remediation is not a DIY project. Without containment, scrubbing or tearing out moldy material releases spores by the millions, potentially spreading contamination through the house and making the health exposure worse than it was. Our crews work in personal protective equipment inside a negative-pressure enclosure, with HEPA air scrubbers running, to trap spores and hold them in the work area while removal is underway.

A professional job goes after the moisture source too, because that is why the mold grew in the first place. If the moisture is left alone, taking away what you can see only buys time, and regrowth often shows up within weeks. Our certified technicians are trained and equipped to track down moisture you cannot see, dry the affected areas the right way and make the corrections that keep mold from recurring.
